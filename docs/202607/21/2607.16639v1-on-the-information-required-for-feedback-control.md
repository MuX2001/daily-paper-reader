## 摘要
不同尺度的生物系统，以及许多工程问题，必须在有限信息下控制噪声系统。在这里，我们研究了随机系统的信息受限反馈控制以实现目标稳态，并推导了从受控系统到控制器的信息率下界。该框架使我们能够获得广泛控制问题在有限信息下的性能-信息帕累托前沿。对于具有状态无关被动动力学的系统，该下界通过一个显式最优控制协议达到，该协议概率性地时间反转被动动力学。我们通过非线性粒子定位、微生物导航和实验实现的信息引擎等应用展示了这些结果。

## Abstract
Biological systems across scales, along with many engineering problems, must control noisy systems with limited information. Here we study information-limited feedback control of stochastic systems to achieve target steady states, and derive a lower bound on the information rate from controlled system to controller. This framework allows us to obtain performance-information Pareto frontiers for wide-ranging control problems with limited information. For systems with state-independent passive dynamics, the bound is saturated by an explicit optimal control protocol which probabilistically time-reverses the passive dynamics. We showcase these results through applications to nonlinear particle localization, microbial navigation, and experimentally realized information engines.
