## 摘要
我们开发了一种在线控制方法，能够与一般类别的因果策略竞争，超越了大多数现有算法所使用的线性控制器类别。在T轮的时间范围内，我们考虑一个已知的线性动态系统，该系统受到对抗性干扰，并且在每次行动后揭示凸成本。该方法在已揭示的历史上模拟基准策略，利用它们的反事实状态-输入对形成移动参考，并在物理系统上应用一个固定的稳定控制器来跟踪该参考。我们将这种方法称为反事实跟踪。反事实跟踪适用于任何可测的因果策略类别，这些策略可以从已揭示的历史中模拟，并且其反事实状态-输入对在每一轮中具有有界直径。策略可以是非线性或动态的，并且不需要共享参数化。我们建立了PAC-Bayes遗憾保证，该保证对每个策略的后验都成立，并取决于其与选定先验的相对熵。在具有有界脉冲响应增益的跟踪器的固定工厂上，一个包含N个策略的有限类别在log N = O(T)时，具有关于T和N的极小极大最优的√(T log N)依赖性。作为一个核心应用，我们与一个稳定线性动态控制器的系统级响应球竞争。该球限制了与固定跟踪器的累积脉冲响应偏差，但没有施加共同的衰变包络、记忆长度或控制器阶数界限。据我们所知，这是第一个在此类别上一致的在线控制保证。一个匹配的下界表明，我们的保证在常数范围内是紧的。

## Abstract
We develop a method for online control that competes with general classes of causal policies, beyond the linear-controller classes used by most existing algorithms. Over a horizon of \(T\) rounds, we consider a known linear dynamical system subject to adversarial disturbances and convex costs revealed after each action. The method simulates the benchmark policies on the revealed history, uses their counterfactual state--input pairs to form a moving reference, and applies a fixed stabilizing controller to track that reference on the physical system. We call this method \emph{counterfactual tracking}.   Counterfactual tracking applies to any measurable class of causal policies that can be simulated from the revealed history and whose counterfactual state--input pairs have bounded diameter at every round. The policies may be nonlinear or dynamic and need not share a parameterization. We establish PAC-Bayes regret guarantees that hold for every posterior over policies and depend on its relative entropy to a chosen prior. On a fixed plant with a tracker of bounded impulse-response gain, a finite class of \(N\) policies admits the minimax-optimal \(\sqrt{T\log N}\) dependence on \(T\) and \(N\) when \(\log N=O(T)\).   As a central application, we compete with a system-level response ball of stabilizing linear dynamical controllers. The ball bounds the summed impulse-response deviation from the fixed tracker, but imposes no common decay envelope, memory length, or controller-order bound. To our knowledge, this is the first online-control guarantee uniform over such a class. A matching lower bound shows that our guarantee is tight up to constants.
