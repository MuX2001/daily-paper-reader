## 摘要
本文提出一种基于数据的方法，用于为有界扰动下的离散时间线性系统设计状态反馈。对于每个容许反馈增益和标量设计参数，Lyapunov 方程确定一个不变椭球：即在允许扰动下状态无法离开的区域。我们优化增益和参数，以最小化所得输出包络的基于迹的度量。在每个参数值处，值迭代给出成本下界，而单独的控制器评估给出可达上界。这些界还使我们能够评估整个参数区间，而无需评估每个点。我们证明，搜索在有限多次评估后停止，并得到一个镇定状态反馈增益，其目标值与所选椭球族上的下确界之差在任意给定绝对容差内。这里既不假设初始增益是镇定的，也不假设下确界可达。该保证假设精确算术以及一批信息充分的精确测量，其中包括数据采集期间的扰动；所得反馈仅使用状态。一个位置-速度示例展示了这些界、控制器校验和计算成本。

## Abstract
This letter develops a data-based method for designing state feedback for a discrete-time linear system under bounded disturbances. For each admissible feedback gain and scalar design parameter, a Lyapunov equation determines an invariant ellipsoid: a region that the state cannot leave under the permitted disturbances. We optimise the gain and parameter to minimise a trace-based measure of the resulting output enclosure. At each parameter value, value iteration gives a lower cost bound, while separate controller evaluation gives an achievable upper bound. These bounds also let us assess whole parameter intervals without evaluating every point. We prove that the search stops after finitely many evaluations with a stabilising state-feedback gain whose objective is within any prescribed absolute tolerance of the infimum over the chosen ellipsoid family. Neither an initially stabilising gain nor attainment of the infimum is assumed. The guarantee assumes exact arithmetic and a sufficiently informative batch of exact measurements, including disturbances during data collection; the resulting feedback uses only the state. A position--velocity example illustrates the bounds, controller checks, and computational cost.
