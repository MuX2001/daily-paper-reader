## 摘要
磁导航为机器人系统提供了非接触且不依赖视线的反馈，但现有方法通常依赖显式位姿估计或直接使用原始磁测量，使精确控制容易受到建模误差、测量噪声和扰动的影响。本文提出 MagServo，一种基于分层学习的框架，可直接利用学习到的潜在磁特征实现鲁棒的六自由度磁伺服。MagServo 通过掩码重建学习抗不确定性的磁表示，并在无需解析磁模型或显式雅可比监督的情况下，捕捉机器人运动与潜在磁变化之间的状态相关交互动力学。基于学习到的动力学，分层控制器将用于粗接近的非线性模型预测控制与用于精确细调节的局部雅可比反演相结合。大量物理实验表明，其达到了亚毫米和亚度级精度，在六自由度位姿到达任务中平均终端误差为 0.386 mm 和 0.479 度。MagServo 在复杂轨迹跟踪中还优于基于定位的控制基线，并在无需重新训练的情况下，对未见过的磁源配置保持鲁棒性能。真实机器人实验的补充视频见 https://youtu.be/rZt1NUP1Mr0。

## Abstract
Magnetic navigation provides contact-free and line-of-sight-independent feedback for robotic systems, yet existing approaches typically rely on explicit pose estimation or direct use of raw magnetic measurements, making accurate control susceptible to modeling errors, measurement noise, and disturbances. This work presents MagServo, a hierarchical learning-based framework for robust 6-DoF magnetic servoing directly using the learned latent magnetic feature. MagServo learns uncertainty-resilient magnetic representations through masked reconstruction and captures state-dependent interaction dynamics between robot motion and latent magnetic transitions without analytical magnetic models or explicit Jacobian supervision. Based on the learned dynamics, a hierarchical controller combines nonlinear model predictive control for coarse approach with local Jacobian inversion for precise fine regulation. Extensive physical experiments demonstrate submillimeter and subdegree accuracy, achieving mean terminal errors of 0.386 mm and 0.479 degree for 6-DoF pose reaching. MagServo further outperforms a localization-based control baseline in complex trajectory tracking and maintains robust performance under unseen magnetic-source configurations without retraining. A supplementary video of the real-robot experiments is available at https://youtu.be/rZt1NUP1Mr0.
