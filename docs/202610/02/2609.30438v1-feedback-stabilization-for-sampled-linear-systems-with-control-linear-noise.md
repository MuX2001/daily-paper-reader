## 摘要
本文研究具有控制线性噪声和周期采样测量的线性随机系统。我们处理均方意义下的反馈镇定问题。本文的主要贡献是给出反馈镇定的一个充分必要条件。特别地，我们将该随机系统的反馈可镇定性与其Riccati型代数方程不动点的存在性联系起来，并进一步与无限时域最优控制问题解的存在性联系起来。

## Abstract
In this paper, we consider linear stochastic systems with control-linear noise and periodically sampled measurements. We address the problem of feedback stabilization in the mean-square sense. The main contribution of the paper is to provide a necessary and sufficient condition for feedback stabilization. In particular, we relate feedback stabilizability of the stochastic system to the existence of a fixed point of a Riccati-type algebraic equation and, further, to the existence of a solution to an infinite-horizon optimal control problem.
