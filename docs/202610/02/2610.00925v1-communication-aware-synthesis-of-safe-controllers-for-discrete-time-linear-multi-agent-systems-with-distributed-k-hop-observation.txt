# Communication-aware Synthesis of Safe Controllers for Discrete-Time Linear Multi-Agent Systems with Distributed k-Hop Observation* 

Yihan Liu 1, Teng Yan 1, Meiqi Tian 1 and Bingzhuo Zhong 1

Abstract — This paper studies communication-aware safe con-trol for discrete-time linear multi-agent systems under limited information exchange. The main challenge lies in the coupling between remote-state estimation and safe controller design, since estimation errors affect the state evolution through the controller gains, while the controller design must account for the resulting observer-induced state perturbations to guarantee safety. To address this challenge, a distributed k-hop observer is developed to reconstruct unavailable remote states, and a uniform observer-error bound is derived. The effect of the observer-induced state perturbation on closed-loop safety is accounted for in both the construction of local εi-robust safe in-variant (RSI) sets and the enforcement of pairwise relative-state safety constraints. The resulting safety conditions ensure that all agents remain within their local RSI sets while all pairwise relative-state safety constraints are satisfied. A linear matrix inequality (LMI)-based optimization method is developed to jointly synthesize the distributed observers, local controllers, and RSI sets. A case study illustrates the effectiveness of the proposed method. 

I. I NTRODUCTION 

Multi-agent systems have been widely used in networked robotics, intelligent transportation, and other cyber-physical applications, where multiple agents are required to cooperate safely. In these applications, large-scale multi-agent systems are deployed over communication networks with limited bandwidth, sparse interconnections, and distributed decision-making architectures. Under these settings, the design of control strategies must account for communication con-straints, making communication-aware control important in networked systems [1]–[4]. To cope with the communication limitations on inter-agent information exchange, distributed estimation and observer design have been extensively studied in networked control systems. Representative works include decentralized state estimation for distributed discrete-time systems [5], pinning-based observer design for networked dynamical systems [6], distributed output regulation for heterogeneous multi-agent systems [7], and consensus-based observer design for multi-agent systems [8]. Recently, [9] developed a distributed framework for k-hop control in large-scale networks, and [10] further proposed a communication-aware strategy based

However, existing methods mainly focus on estimation convergence and closed-loop stability, while the impact of observer-induced pertur-bations on safety guarantees has received less attention. In parallel, safe controller synthesis has been extensively studied using invariant-set and robust safety methods [11]– [13]. Recently, [14] developed a direct data-driven method for synthesizing safety controllers for uncertain linear sys-tems, [15] proposed a set-based approach for formal con-troller synthesis, and [16] presented a secure-by-construction framework that enforces safety and security properties. A ro-bust safe invariant (RSI) synthesis framework was developed in [17] by incorporating communication-induced estimation errors into controller design. But in multi-agent systems, such estimation errors can also arise from the reconstruction of remote-agent states that are unavailable through direct communication. Under limited inter-agent communication, where each agent can directly exchange information only with its 1-hop neighbors, the remote states required by the local feedback controller are not directly available and must therefore be reconstructed by distributed observers. The resulting estimation errors induced by observers affect the state evolution through the controller gains, thereby coupling the observer and controller designs. This paper develops a communication-aware framework for safe controller synthesis of multi-agent systems with distributed k-hop observation, where direct information ex-change is restricted to 1-hop neighbors and the states of agents 2 to k hops away are reconstructed by distributed observers. The resulting estimation errors are propagated through the controller gains to characterize the observer-induced state perturbation, whose effect is incorporated into local robust safe invariant (RSI) sets and pairwise relative-state safety constraints. An LMI-based framework is then developed to jointly synthesize the distributed observers and safe controllers while enforcing local RSI, pairwise relative-state safety, and input constraints. The main contributions of this paper are summarized as follows. 1) We construct a distributed k-hop observer for discrete-time linear multi-agent systems, and establish the observer-error dynamics and a uniform error bound. 2) We characterize the observer-induced state perturbation caused by remote-state estimation and incorporate its effect on closed-loop safety into the synthesis condi-tions. 3) We develop an LMI-based optimization framework to arXiv:2610.00925v1 [eess.SY] 1 Oct 2026 jointly synthesize the observers and controllers while accounting for their coupling and ensuring local state invariance, relative-state safety, and input constraints. CONCLUSION This paper studied communication-aware safe controller synthesis for discrete-time linear multi-agent systems. A distributed k-hop observer was developed to reconstruct unavailable remote states, and uniform observer-error bounds were derived to characterize the resulting observer-induced state perturbations. The neighboring and remote states in-volved in each local controller were incorporated through an augmented admissible-state domain, based on which local εi-RSI and relative-state safety conditions were established. An LMI-based optimization framework was developed to jointly synthesize the distributed observers, safe state-feedback con-trollers, and local RSI sets while accounting for the observer– controller coupling. The proposed framework provides a link between communication-aware estimation and safety-guaranteed control for multi-agent systems. APPENDIX
