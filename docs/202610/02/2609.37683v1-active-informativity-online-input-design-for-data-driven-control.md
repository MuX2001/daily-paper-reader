## 摘要
我们提出了一个统一框架，用于从含噪数据进行数据驱动控制中的在线输入设计。我们引入了一种性能度量，并分析了随着新数据的加入，这种质量度量如何变化。基于该分析，我们提出了一种在线选择输入的方法，以确保数据质量不会随时间下降。我们通过将六个常见的含噪数据驱动控制问题纳入我们的框架，并对其中三个问题求解数值算例，验证了该方法的有效性。

## Abstract
We develop a unified framework for online input design in data-driven control from noisy data. We introduce a performance measure and analyze how such quality measure changes as new data are added. Based on such analysis we propose an online method for selecting inputs that ensures that data quality does not decrease over time. We demonstrate the validity of our approach by formulating six common noisy data-driven control problems in our framework, and by solving numerical examples for three of them.
