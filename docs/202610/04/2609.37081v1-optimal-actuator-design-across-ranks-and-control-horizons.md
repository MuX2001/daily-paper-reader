## Abstract
We study how actuator rank and control horizon jointly determine worst-case control performance for finite-dimensional linear systems under a fixed total actuator-gain budget. The design variable is the input covariance $X=BB^\top$. Scalar actuators give rank-one matrices $X=bb^\top$; dropping the rank constraint yields their convex hull and a semidefinite benchmark. We identify regimes in which low-rank designs necessarily fall short and others in which they attain the benchmark. At small time, the relaxed maximizer is unique and full rank.
