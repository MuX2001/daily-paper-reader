## 摘要
与通常并非最优的“对象—控制—估计器”顺序设计流程不同，我们提出控制与估计协同设计（ContEst）框架，将整个系统设计表述为一个由一阶方法求解的单一两阶段问题。一个关键使能点是：根据包络定理，每个内层控制/估计最优代价关于设计参数的梯度，可由内层求解器已经返回的对偶变量获得：在温和正则性（内层极小值点唯一）下是精确的，在更宽松条件下则作为次梯度。也就是说，无需通过优化器或其最优性条件进行微分。我们首先在线性二次高斯（LQG）情形下给出该框架，其中控制与估计代价成为两个半定规划（SDP），其设计梯度可从Lyapunov约束的对偶量中读取。我们将结果扩展到鲁棒（H∞）形式，以涵盖最坏情况控制与滤波问题。我们还将其扩展到约束与非线性系统，使用扩展卡尔曼滤波器（eKF）和信息状态动力学的局部线性化，得到一个近似但凸的模型预测控制（MPC）问题，其设计梯度可直接从协态中读取。我们将方法应用于来自不同领域的多种实际设计示例，并报告了显著的设计改进。

## Abstract
Instead of the sequential plant--control--estimator design pipeline, which is in general not optimal, we propose the Control and Estimation Co-Design (ContEst) framework, which poses the entire system design as a single two-stage problem solved by first-order methods. A key enabler is that the gradient of each inner control/estimation optimal cost with respect to the design parameters is available from the dual variables the inner solver already returns, by the envelope theorem: exactly under mild regularity (a unique inner minimizer), and as a subgradient under more relaxed conditions. That is, no differentiation through the optimizer or its optimality conditions is needed. We first present the framework in a linear quadratic Gaussian (LQG) regime, where the control and estimation costs become two semidefinite programs (SDPs) whose design gradients are read from the duals of the Lyapunov constraints. We extend the results to robust ($H_\infty$) formulation to cover worst-case control and filtering problems. We also present an extension to constrained and nonlinear systems, using an extended Kalman filter (eKF) and a local linearization of the information-state dynamics to yield an approximate but convex model predictive control (MPC) problem whose design gradients are read directly from the costates. We apply our methods to a variety of realistic design examples from different fields and report significant design improvements.
