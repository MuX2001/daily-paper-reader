## 摘要
在网络化马尔可夫决策过程（MDP）中，转移动态通常未知，且状态—动作空间随智能体数量快速增长。在此设定下，泰勒表示可以自然地逼近 $Q$ 函数，但对 $N$ 个智能体进行朴素的 $n$ 阶展开需要 $Θ(N^n)$ 个系数。我们在期望未来局部奖励光滑且导数受控的条件下，为这些展开提供了依据。在该条件下，有限速度的信息传播与折扣表明，局部评论家（local-critic）的泰勒系数随到所涉及最远智能体的图距离呈指数衰减。丢弃远距离智能体的系数并进行边缘化，可得到可扩展的局部泰勒表示，其误差界由图局部性控制。基于这些表示，我们提出了一种可扩展的无模型 actor--critic 算法，并为线性 LSTD 评论家建立了有限样本评论家保证和近平稳性保证。随后，我们引入了一种表达能力更强的神经 TD 参数化。与先前的构造性谱方法不同，我们的方法适用于无法获得已知局部动态映射的场景，例如隐藏切换线性—二次调节。在三个控制基准上，我们的方法匹配或优于谱基线，同时能高效扩展到大规模图。

## Abstract
In Networked Markov Decision Processes, transition dynamics are often unknown and the state--action space grows rapidly with the number of agents. In this setting, Taylor representations naturally approximate $Q$-functions, but a naive order-$n$ expansion over $N$ agents requires $Θ(N^n)$ coefficients. We justify these expansions under smooth expected future local rewards with controlled derivatives. Under this condition, finite-speed information propagation and discounting imply that local-critic Taylor coefficients decay exponentially with the graph distance to the farthest agent involved. Discarding distant-agent coefficients and marginalizing then yield scalable local Taylor representations with a bound controlled by graph locality. Building on these representations, we propose a scalable model-free actor--critic algorithm, establishing finite-sample critic and near-stationarity guarantees for a linear LSTD critic. We then introduce a more expressive neural TD parameterization. Unlike prior constructive spectral methods, our approach covers settings without access to a known local dynamics map, such as hidden switched linear--quadratic regulation. Across three control benchmarks, our method matches or outperforms spectral baselines while scaling efficiently to large graphs.
