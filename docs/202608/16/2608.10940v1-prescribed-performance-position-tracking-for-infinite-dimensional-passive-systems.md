## 摘要
我们研究了线性无穷维被动系统的漏斗控制，其受控输出通过积分被动输出获得。在机械应用中，这对应于基于测量的被动速度输出的位置控制。在系统节点框架内，我们提出了一种非线性递归漏斗控制器，带有动态增益监督器，适用于分布或边界控制与观测的系统。每当归一化递归误差接近规定的监测水平时，监督器自动增加阻尼增益。控制器使用跟踪误差及其导数，后者直接从被动输出获得，而非通过数值微分。我们证明了闭环轨迹的全局存在性和唯一性、规定的漏斗性能以及导数跟踪误差和被动输出的全局有界性。在额外的位置兼容严格耗散条件下，监督增益仅有限次增加并最终变为常数。因此，增益、内部状态和控制输入全局有界，且递归误差漏斗裕度随时间一致。结果通过具有均匀正分布阻尼的边界驱动欧拉-伯努利梁进行了说明。

## Abstract
We study funnel control for linear infinite-dimensional passive systems whose controlled output is obtained by integrating the passive output. In mechanical applications, this corresponds to position control based on a measured passive velocity output. Within the system-node framework, we propose a nonlinear recursive funnel controller with a dynamic gain supervisor for systems with distributed or boundary control and observation. The supervisor automatically increases a damping gain whenever the normalized recursive error approaches a prescribed monitoring level. The controller uses the tracking error and its derivative, with the latter obtained directly from the passive output rather than by numerical differentiation. We prove global existence and uniqueness of the closed-loop trajectory, prescribed funnel performance, and global boundedness of the derivative tracking error and the passive output. Under an additional position-compatible strict dissipativity condition, the supervised gain is increased only finitely many times and eventually becomes constant. Consequently, the gain, the internal state, and the control input are globally bounded, and the recursive-error funnel margin is uniform in time. The results are illustrated by a boundary-actuated Euler-Bernoulli beam with uniformly positive distributed damping.
