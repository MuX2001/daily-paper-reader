## 摘要
我们针对具有两种随机源且非马尔可夫随机系数仅适应于其中之一的随机最优控制问题，发展了新的对偶方法。哈密顿-雅可比-贝尔曼（HJB）方程是一个二阶倒向随机偏微分方程。我们的对偶理论通过一个由其中一个布朗运动实现参数化的合适路径wise最优控制问题，提供了随机值函数的替代描述。这使我们能够利用粗糙最优控制理论重新获得马尔可夫性，从而得到合适的粘性意义下求解的粗糙二阶HJB方程。

## Abstract
We develop novel duality methods for stochastic optimal control problems under two sources of randomness and non-Markovian random coefficients adapted to just one of them. The Hamilton-Jacobi-Bellman (HJB) equation is a second-order backward stochastic partial differential equation. Our duality theory provides an alternative description of the random value function in terms of a suitable pathwise optimal control problem parameterized by the realizations of one of the Brownian motions. This allows us to regain Markovianity using the theory of rough optimal control problems, leading to rough second order HJB equations that we solve in a suitable viscosity sense.
