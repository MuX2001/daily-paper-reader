## 摘要
我们考虑任意但固定有限输入输出维数 n 的方阵真实有理被控对象的同时内部稳定化，这些对象被组织为仿射右互质因子段，并在显式的非退化假设下进行。经过经典归一化后，剩余的全局谱割约束以及有限极点处与无穷远点处的重构约束，借助函数级双 Cayley 分解，被无损地编码为一个有限维半代数种子。对于取值于可有效表示的实闭域中的输入系数，该种子给出精确的存在性判定，且无需枚举控制器的 McMillan 阶数；判定引擎为经典量词消去。同一编码，现在遍历有理半径、容许种子以及剩余的幺模和有理 Schur 参数，产生该段所有真实有理公共稳定化控制器的满射图册。这些结果针对这一结构化类，并与三个一般被控对象同时稳定化的有理不可判定性相容。

## Abstract
We consider simultaneous internal stabilization of square proper real-rational plants of arbitrary but fixed finite input-output dimension $n$, arranged as an affine right-coprime factor segment, under explicit nondegeneracy hypotheses. After a classical normalization, the remaining global spectral-cut constraint and the reconstruction constraints at finite poles and at infinity are encoded, without loss, as a finite-dimensional semialgebraic seed, using a function-level double-Cayley factorization. For input coefficients in an effectively presented real closed field, that seed yields an exact existence decision that does not enumerate controller McMillan degree; the decision engine is classical quantifier elimination. The same encoding, now ranging over rational radii, admissible seeds, and residual unimodular and rational Schur parameters, yields a surjective atlas of all proper real-rational common stabilizers of the segment. The results concern this structured class and are compatible with the rational undecidability of simultaneous stabilization of three general plants.
