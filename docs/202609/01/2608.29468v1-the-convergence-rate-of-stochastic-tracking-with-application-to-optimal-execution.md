## 摘要
我们研究一般随机目标过程的二次跟踪问题，其中控制为绝对连续，并考虑有终端约束和无终端约束两种情况。我们推导了以目标的Besov型模量表示的显式非渐近上界。这些界给出了尖锐的显式速率，对于半鞅目标，该速率具体化为平方根阶。然后我们将这些结果应用于具有随机终端库存的广义Obizhaeva--Wang执行模型。我们首先发展一种Hilbert空间方法来刻画其最优策略，该策略包含跳跃。为避免此类交易尖峰，我们通过系数为$\varepsilon$的二次交易速率惩罚对问题进行正则化。然后我们证明正则化最优执行成本——以及正则化最优策略的超额价格冲击成本——以尖锐速率$O(\sqrt{\varepsilon})$收敛。由于正则化最优策略没有闭式解，我们进一步构造了一个易于实现的近似最优策略，该策略具有相同的近似速率。

## Abstract
We study the quadratic tracking problem of a general stochastic target process with absolutely continuous controls, with and without terminal constraint. We derive explicit, non-asymptotic upper bounds in terms of a Besov-type modulus of the target. These bounds yield sharp explicit rates that specialize to the square-root order for semimartingale targets. We then apply these results to a generalized Obizhaeva--Wang execution model with random terminal inventory. We first develop a Hilbert-space approach to characterize its optimal strategy, which includes jumps. To avoid such trading spikes, one regularizes the problem by a quadratic trading-rate penalty with coefficient $\varepsilon$. We then show that the regularized optimal execution cost---and therefore the excess price impact cost of the regularized optimal strategy---converges at the sharp rate $O(\sqrt{\varepsilon})$. Since the regularized optimal strategy is not available in closed form, we further construct a nearly optimal strategy which is readily implementable and shares the same approximation rate.
