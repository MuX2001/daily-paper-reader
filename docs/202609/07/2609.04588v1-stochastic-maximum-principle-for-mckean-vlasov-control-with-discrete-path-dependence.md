## 摘要
我们研究了一类具有离散路径依赖的McKean-Vlasov控制问题。系数和成本泛函可能依赖于受控状态的有限个过去值（在固定的确定性时刻观测）及其联合分布。我们建立了受控状态方程的适定性，并通过随机庞特里亚金最大值原理推导了必要和充分的最优性条件。伴随过程由一个在固定观测时刻具有跳跃的倒向随机微分方程刻画。每个跳跃表示未来成本相对于相应观测状态及其分布的条件敏感性。在线性二次情形中，我们通过结合延拓论证与平均场Riccati约化，证明了所得正倒向系统的全局可解性。最后，我们讨论了在时间序列生成中的应用，其中终端成本由采样路径的律与目标路径分布之间的基于核的差异给出。

## Abstract
We study a class of McKean-Vlasov control problems with discrete path dependence. The coefficients and cost functional may depend on finitely many past values of the controlled state, observed at fixed deterministic times, and on their joint law. We establish well-posedness of the controlled state equation and derive necessary and sufficient optimality conditions through a stochastic Pontryagin maximum principle. The adjoint process is characterized by a backward stochastic differential equation with jumps at fixed observation times. Each jump represents the conditional sensitivity of future costs with respect to the corresponding observed state and its distribution. In the linear-quadratic case, we prove global solvability of the resulting forward-backward system by combining a continuation argument with a mean-field Riccati reduction. We finally discuss an application to time-series generation, where the terminal cost is given by a kernel-based discrepancy between the law of the sampled path and a target path distribution.
