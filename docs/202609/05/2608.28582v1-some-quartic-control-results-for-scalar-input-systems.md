## 摘要
我们研究了标量输入系统的小时间局部可控性中四次项的作用。首先，我们证明了可控性的一个新的充分条件，该条件比之前的结果同时利用更多有利的四次李括号。其次，我们识别了一族可控性的四次障碍，这些障碍依赖于第二类坐标是控制的正定泛函的李括号。第三，我们展示了这些结果的互补性可以看作是对Kawski于1987年提出的一个开放问题的回答，该问题涉及构造一个以某种方式区分好的和坏的四次李括号的Hall基。我们给出了一些例子和评论，以说明这些概念的一些复杂性。

## Abstract
We investigate the role of quartic terms in the small-time local controllability of scalar-input systems. First, we prove a new sufficient condition for controllability, which exploits simultaneously more good quartic Lie brackets than previous results. Second, we identify a family of quartic obstructions to controllability, relying on Lie brackets whose coordinates of the second kind are positive-definite functionals of the control. Third, we show that the complementarity of these results can be seen as an answer to Kawski's 1987 open problem concerning the construction of a Hall basis which somehow separates good and bad quartic brackets. We give examples and remarks illustrating some of the intricacies of these notions.
