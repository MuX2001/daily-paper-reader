## 摘要
本文建立了一个由二维和三维对流Brinkman-Forchheimer方程控制在d维环面（d∈{2,3}）上的最优控制问题的验证定理：  $$\frac{\partial\mathfrak{u}}{\partial t}   -μΔ\mathfrak{u}   +(\mathfrak{u}\cdot\nabla)\mathfrak{u}   +α\mathfrak{u}   +β|\mathfrak{u}|^{r-1}\mathfrak{u}   +\nabla\mathfrak{p}   =\boldsymbol{f},   \qquad   \nabla\cdot\mathfrak{u}=0,$$  其中μ,α,β>0且r∈[1,∞)。我们推导了庞特里亚金最大值原理，并为相关的控制问题开发了一个验证框架，这是一个对于Navier-Stokes型流体模型相对较少受到关注的主题。为CBF系统建立验证定理和相应的反馈刻画的一个主要挑战是，分析所需的正则性框架与用于二维Navier-Stokes方程的框架有本质不同。特别是，本方法依赖于强解理论、对非线性吸收项的精细处理、负阶Sobolev空间中的新估计以及更强拓扑下的连续依赖性估计，尤其是在三维情形下。本工作的一个显著特点是验证框架不仅在二维中建立，还在三维超临界区域（对应r∈(3,5]）以及临界情形r=3且满足条件2βμ≥1时建立。因此，反馈刻画和验证论证在二维和三维中都可以严格证明。

## Abstract
This article establishes a verification theorem for an optimal control problem governed by the two- and three-dimensional convective Brinkman--Forchheimer equations on the $d$-dimensional torus, $d\in\{2,3\}$:   $$\frac{\partial\mathfrak{u}}{\partial t}   -μΔ\mathfrak{u}   +(\mathfrak{u}\cdot\nabla)\mathfrak{u}   +α\mathfrak{u}   +β|\mathfrak{u}|^{r-1}\mathfrak{u}   +\nabla\mathfrak{p}   =\boldsymbol{f},   \qquad   \nabla\cdot\mathfrak{u}=0,$$   where $μ,α,β>0$ and $r\in[1,\infty)$. We derive the Pontryagin maximum principle and develop a verification framework for the associated control problem, a topic that has received comparatively little attention for fluid models of Navier--Stokes type. A major challenge in establishing the verification theorem and the corresponding feedback characterization for the CBF system is that the analysis requires a substantially different regularity framework from that used for the two-dimensional Navier--Stokes equations. In particular, the present approach relies on strong solution theory, a delicate treatment of the nonlinear absorption term, novel estimates in negative-order Sobolev spaces, and continuous dependence estimates in stronger topologies, especially in the three-dimensional setting. A distinctive feature of the present work is that the verification framework is developed not only in two dimensions, but also in the three-dimensional supercritical regime, corresponding to $r\in(3,5]$, and in the critical case $r=3$ under the condition $2βμ\geq1$. Consequently, the feedback characterization and verification arguments can be rigorously justified in both two and three dimensions.
